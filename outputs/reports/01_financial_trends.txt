DIRECT ANSWER
Net transaction revenue increased from 180,419,443.08 to 189,710,371.80, an increase of 9,290,928.72 (+5.1496%).
Recorded flight expenses increased from 192,985,563.99 to 238,376,476.25, an increase of 45,390,912.26 (+23.5204%).

Proxy margin changed from -6.9649% to -25.6528%, down 18.6879 percentage points.

WHEN
The measured proxy first becomes negative in March 2025 (-1,014,626.60), following February 2025 +592,799.55.
It remains negative in all 18 months March 2025-Aug 2026; August 2025 -4,494,761.76. Fiscal-quarter and monthly CSVs contain complete exact evidence.
February 2025 observed flights=792; March 2025=1,157 (+46.09%). Spring capacity growth coincides with the proxy crossing below zero. This timing agrees broadly with spring expansion described in case package, but purchase/flight date mismatch prevents assigning causal flight profit.

WHY (DESCRIPTIVE, NOT CAUSAL)
45,390,912.26 additional expenses overwhelmed 9,290,928.72 additional net revenue.
Expense increases: Fuel 16,121,247.14; Crew 15,049,420.86; Maintenance 13,003,356.24; Food 1,216,888.02.
Fuel+crew account for 68.6716% of expense growth. Maintenance is also material.
Ticket transaction revenue increased 10,145,584.72; signed Refund amount changed -1,142,771.50; other transaction changes Change Fee +45,300.00, Extra Baggage +135,542.05, Food & Onboard +57,300.73, Seat Upgrade +49,972.72.
Observed flight count increased from 13,072 to 15,729 (+20.3259%). Every route_id+cost_date key has exactly 4 rows and 4 distinct expense categories (28,801 observed flights across both years).
Average recorded expense per flight increased from 14,763.28 to 15,155.22 (+2.6549%).
Index decomposition: extra flights valued at FY1 mean expense = 39,226,028.42 (86.42% of added expense); changed mean expense at FY2 flight count = 6,164,883.84. Sums exactly to expense increase before display rounding. The latter combines price, route and season mix; neither term proves causal impact.
Ticket transaction counts 178,217 → 187,444 (+5.1774%); mean ticket amount 1029.233499 → 1032.695051 (+0.3363%). These are not unique passengers, load factors or departure-linked fares.

No flight-date revenue, passenger counts, seats, fuel quantity/price, crew hours/rates or rewards liability/cost data.
Gate leases excluded here and handled by notebook 05 because periods differ and 2026-27 lease data missing.
