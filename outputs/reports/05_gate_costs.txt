NorthByte's gate leases increased from CAD 9,664,400.00 in 2024–2025 (calendar 2024) to CAD 9,877,700.00 in 2025–2026 (calendar 2025): CAD 213,300.00 more, or +2.21%. The portfolio remained at 34 gates across 20 airports. All gate prices rose; the four hubs account for 67.37% of the increase. Each gate is counted once, avoiding CAD 7,941,900 of overstatement from the latest route-assignment rows.

For the missing 2026–2027 lease year (January 1, 2026 to January 1, 2027, end exclusive), budget approximately CAD 10.096 million. Repeating each gate's observed percentage increase on the same portfolio produces CAD 10,095,844.04, up CAD 218,144.04 (+2.21%). Sensitivities are CAD 9,877,700.00 if rates stay flat, CAD 10,091,000.00 if dollar increases repeat, and CAD 10,095,707.68 if the portfolio-wide percentage repeats. The scenario span of CAD 9.878–10.096 million is not a confidence interval or a guaranteed contractual range. Only one annual change is observed; gate additions, closures, and negotiated terms could move actual spending outside it.

Inefficiency review: Halifax A3 and Ottawa A2 have no assigned routes in either recorded lease year. They cost CAD 433,500.00 (4.39% of calendar-2025 leases), versus CAD 425,700.00 previously (+1.83%); the primary calendar-2026 forecast carries CAD 441,444.87 for them. Review sharing, consolidation, and release at renewal, after confirming occupancy, contingency and community-service needs. These amounts are potential spending exposure, not assured savings. Actual departures rose 20.33%; the available counts do not measure gate throughput capacity or prove congestion/idle time.

Allocation and fiscal timing: Follow the case rule: allocate each airport's total gate cost evenly across its departing flights, then aggregate to routes; add no arrival charge. Keep the route-assigned lease pool and unassigned-gate overhead visible and charge each pool once. On uniform monthly accrual, FY2024–25 is estimated at CAD 9,806,600.00, including CAD 430,900.00 of unassigned overhead. FY2025–26 is estimated at CAD 10,023,129.36, including CAD 438,796.58 of unassigned overhead and eight months of forecast rates. Allocations reconcile exactly to airport totals. Estimated gate cost per departure falls from CAD 750.20 to CAD 637.24 (-15.06%) as departures increase from 13,072 to 15,729. These are allocation estimates, not audited expenses or flight operating contribution. The practical priority is to reconstruct missing lease contracts, plan around CAD 10.10 million, and review unassigned gate exposure while protecting required operations.
